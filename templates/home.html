Soybean Nodule PTM Viewer

Soybean PTM Viewer is a centralized resource for soybean post-translational modifications (PTMs) designed for both wet-lab and dry-lab scientists.

Use intuitive modules to search proteins, discover PTM motifs, browse curated entries, and perform list-level PTM analysis.

The platform supports data submission and offers expandable modules so your team can add more pages later without refactoring the core layout.

Features

Protein Search

Search soybean or rhizobium proteins by gene name, UniProt accession, short identifier, or exact protein sequence.

The result page links PTM records to the annotated protein sequence, highlights modified residues, summarizes site counts, and provides downloadable site tables for follow-up analysis.

  • Supports fuzzy identifier matching and multi-ID batch queries.
  • Displays residue-level PTM sites with sequence context and quantification charts.

PTM Search

Query PTM patterns across curated soybean nodule datasets with motif-style expressions such as single modifications, alternative modifications, or combined crosstalk events.

This module helps identify candidate regulatory sites by PTM type, amino acid position, surrounding sequence pattern, and protein annotation.

  • Finds PTM motifs and co-occurring modification patterns.
  • Filters results by species, PTM type, peptide uniqueness, and representative protein records.

PTM BLAST

Paste a protein sequence and compare it against the local PTM-enabled protein database to find homologous proteins with known modification sites.

Aligned hits preserve PTM annotations so conserved or shifted modification positions can be inspected directly in the protein alignment context.

  • Combines sequence similarity search with PTM-aware hit summaries.
  • Useful for transferring candidate PTM evidence across related proteins or families.

List Analysis

Analyze submitted protein lists against the PTM database to summarize modification abundance, affected protein counts, and PTM-type distribution.

The workflow is designed for proteomics result interpretation, helping compare candidate protein sets with the background PTM collection in this viewer.

  • Summarizes PTM counts and enrichment-style signals for protein groups.
  • Exports tables and graphics for reports, manuscripts, or downstream scripts.
